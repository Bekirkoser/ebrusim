How to Use

Use your mouse or turn on Camera

Move the Paint

Hold the left mouse button and drag. With Camera, pinch thumb + index finger and move.

Drop Paint

Right-click once to add a drop in your selected color. With Camera, pinch thumb + middle finger.

Pick a Color

Choose a swatch or make a custom color in the Paint Color palette.

Customize

Use the panel on the right to tune the flow, background, and capture.
